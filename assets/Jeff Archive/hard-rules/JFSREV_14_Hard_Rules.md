# @jfsrev — 14 Hard Rules (checklist)

Source: Jeff’s Oct 21, 2025 post + later X clarifications.  
Original: https://x.com/jfsrev/status/1980639074680729612  
Images saved under `/workspace/jfsrev/hard-rules/`.

Framing (his words): non-negotiables for discipline, expansive RRR under controlled risk, and managing gap-down losses. **Execution quality > setup rating** — an A setup with C execution is still a C trade.

---

## Pre-trade filters (don’t even arm the order)

| # | Hard rule | Plain English |
|---|-----------|---------------|
| **1** | No entry if **LoD already exceeds ~60% of ATR** | Stop distance (to low-of-day / structure) is already too wide vs the stock’s daily volatility. Visual: ATR as “spring coil.” |
| **2** | No entry if **ATR% from 50-MA > ~4×** | Too extended from the 50-day. Visual: ATR% from 50-MA as “launchpad.” Selectivity is the edge. |
| **3** | **Biotechs out** of post-market scan (gap risk). Exposure only via **IBB/XBI** through **LABU/LABD**. | Kill binary overnight gap risk in single-name biotechs. |
| **4** | No entry without **substantial RVOL** (except mega-cap liquid WL / liquid ETFs) | Outside RVOL, price fades. Volume = participation filter. |
| **5** | **Delay ~30 min** after the open unless **extreme RVOL** already printing | First half-hour is expensive; his ORH-at-open dataset underperformed. |
| **6** | No entry ahead of **major econ data** or **pre/post earnings** | Needs duration cushion; clean chart ≠ enough. |
| **7** | No long **against a declining 200-MA** | Don’t fight the higher-timeframe trend. |
| **8** | No entry into an **immediate gap resistance** zone | Don’t buy into overhead gap fill / resistance right at entry. |

## Session / portfolio rules (how he runs the day)

| # | Hard rule | Plain English |
|---|-----------|---------------|
| **9** | **≤ 3 new positions per session** | His data: ~87% of trades after #3 were losers; winners past that didn’t pay for the losers. Roll risk as prior-day entries confirm. |
| **10** | **Never chase** — even one day late. Only optimal entry. | “Missing trades won’t make you broke; chasing often will.” |
| **11** | The more the market rises **consecutive days**, the more cautious on new longs | Crowd / extension caution after multi-day up legs. |
| **12** | Once a **durable book** (beyond T+3), prioritize **adds to winners** already above breakeven / proven leaders | Scale into what’s working, not new lottery tickets. |
| **13** | Extremely cautious **layering risk on gap-up opens** | Don’t stack fresh risk when the open is already extended overnight. |
| **14** | Always express ideas as **longs** — shorts via **inverse / synthetic long ETFs** | Structural long bias; weakness played with inverse products, not naked short book. |

## How this plugs into entry (one flow)

1. Name passes selection (liquid, high ADR%, RS) →  
2. Passes **#1–8** filters →  
3. Wait **#5** / volume → sniper entry (tight LoD vs ATR) →  
4. Obey **#9–13** on size/frequency/adds →  
5. Express via **#14** product choice.
